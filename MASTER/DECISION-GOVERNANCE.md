# My Bali — Decision and Documentation Governance

## Status labels
Use these labels consistently:
- **Vision:** enduring direction or intended purpose.
- **Observation:** something noticed in real use or the market.
- **Idea:** an untested possibility.
- **Proposal:** an option recommended for discussion.
- **Assumption:** something temporarily believed but not validated.
- **Decision:** explicitly accepted direction.
- **Validation task:** evidence or testing still required.
- **Deferred:** intentionally postponed.
- **Rejected:** considered and not selected, with rationale where useful.

## Rules
1. Do not turn an illustrative example into an approved requirement.
2. Do not label a proposal as a decision before founder confirmation.
3. Revise the relevant existing document when a thought develops; avoid repetitive append-only notes.
4. Record meaningful changes in CHANGE-REGISTER.md.
5. For material changes, summarize the interpretation and ask the founder to confirm before changing core direction.
6. Keep internal planning distinct from public claims.
7. The public website must accurately reflect what is available today.
8. Never claim a feature is implemented, deployed, verified, or operational without evidence.
9. Technical design should follow the agreed product behavior and must account for security, privacy, legal compliance, accessibility, and operational cost.
