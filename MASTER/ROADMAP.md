# My Bali — Product Roadmap (Living Outline)

**Status:** Directional sequence only. No delivery dates or feature commitments have been approved.

## Stage 0 — Foundation and discovery
- Establish product identity, repository, documentation structure, and public introduction.
- Consolidate founder vision, user/provider journeys, marketplace principles, and open questions.
- Validate core user problems and provider willingness to participate.
- Define privacy, safety, age suitability, moderation, and trust principles.

## Stage 1 — Useful first experience
- Ensure the first user has meaningful discovery and planning options before a large community exists.
- Decide the source and maintenance process for destination information.
- Define user profile, interests, saved places, and private planning.
- Establish the minimum viable provider onboarding and verification process.
- Avoid fake activity, fabricated social proof, and empty-feed dead ends.

## Stage 2 — Provider presence and discovery
- Allow eligible direct providers to establish a verified presence.
- Support accurate business details, current pricing, photos/videos, and update dates.
- Design provider pages, announcements, followers, likes, and relevant content discovery.
- Establish content ranking and sponsored-content transparency.

## Stage 3 — Booking and transaction foundation
- Design booking lifecycle, payment integration, order records, cancellation/refund handling, and provider reconciliation.
- Design QR or equivalent booking validation and redemption.
- Pilot with a small number of genuine providers before broader rollout.

## Stage 4 — Travel record and community
- Add evidence-aware travel records and privacy controls.
- Support public stories and useful discussions without treating plans as completed visits.
- Add reporting, moderation, anti-spam, and appeals.
- Evaluate messaging and live capabilities only when safety and operations can support them.

## Stage 5 — Operational scale
- Establish staff roles, provider support, customer support, moderation operations, incident response, analytics, and service-level expectations.
- Build a sustainable revenue model and infrastructure budget.
- Delegate through SOPs and team ownership.

## Roadmap discipline
Priorities and stages must be revised using founder discussion, user validation, provider feedback, feasibility, risk, cost, and actual operational capacity. A roadmap entry is not an authorization to build.
