# My Bali — Open Questions and Validation Register

Questions are not decisions. Resolve them only with evidence and explicit founder confirmation.

## Product identity and governance
- [ ] Confirm public product naming, trademark, domain, and app-store availability.
- [ ] Determine legal/company structure and relationship to Bagusin.
- [ ] Define governance, ownership, and treatment of founder-affiliated providers.

## Local-first strategy and launch
- [ ] Validate the highest-frequency needs of Bali residents and choose the first real service pilot.
- [ ] Define how to measure trust, repeat use, availability, service quality, and local provider value.
- [ ] Define how domestic and international visitors are served without displacing resident-first priorities.
- [ ] Validate competitive differentiation against existing mobility, delivery, food, shopping, and travel platforms.

## Product family and category scope
- [ ] Confirm taxonomy and launch scope for My Bali Food, Shopping, Kurir, Taxi, Spa, Trip, Stay, Rental, Activity, Event, Service, Lifestyle, and possible Property.
- [ ] Decide product categories allowed in My Bali Shopping; used goods and peer-to-peer vehicle listings are outside the intended core scope.
- [ ] Decide whether gadget sales and other higher-risk retail categories are permitted.
- [ ] Define category-specific workflows rather than forcing every service into one generic checkout.

## First-user and cold-start experience
- [ ] Which destination information can My Bali publish before providers register?
- [ ] Who maintains destination accuracy, prices, opening hours, and update dates?
- [ ] What can the first user do with no providers and no community posts?
- [ ] How should new-user interest onboarding work, and how can preferences be changed?
- [ ] What is the distinction between curated information, official provider content, user posts, and sponsored placement?
