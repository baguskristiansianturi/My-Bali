# My Bali — Open Questions and Validation Register

Questions are not decisions. Resolve them only with evidence and explicit founder confirmation.

## Product identity and governance
- [ ] Confirm public product naming, trademark, domain, and app-store availability.
- [ ] Determine legal/company structure and relationship to Bagusin.
- [ ] Define governance, ownership, and treatment of founder-affiliated providers.

## First-user and cold-start experience
- [ ] Which destination information can My Bali publish before providers register?
- [ ] Who maintains destination accuracy, prices, opening hours, and update dates?
- [ ] What can the first user do with no providers and no community posts?
- [ ] How should new-user interest onboarding work, and how can preferences be changed?
- [ ] What is the distinction between curated information, official provider content, user posts, and sponsored placement?

## Provider ecosystem
- [ ] Define owner/authorized representative evidence and category-specific verification.
- [ ] Decide treatment of agents, resellers, franchises, property managers, and multi-branch businesses.
- [ ] Confirm whether basic provider listing and management are free and under what conditions.
- [ ] Decide fees, commission, optional tools, and paid promotion.
- [ ] Define content publishing, follower, engagement, frequency, and ranking rules.
- [ ] Create quality, complaint, warning, restriction, removal, and appeal SOPs.

## Booking and payment
- [ ] Define booking lifecycle, inventory, scheduling, and provider synchronization.
- [ ] Select payment gateway and settlement model.
- [ ] Define refunds, cancellations, disputes, chargebacks, and provider payout.
- [ ] Design secure QR issuance, scan validation, duplicate redemption prevention, offline handling, and audit trail.
- [ ] Determine how payment status, booking status, and actual service fulfillment remain distinct.

## Social experience and trust
- [ ] Define what can be posted without a transaction and what requires evidence.
- [ ] Design public web sharing, app deep links, and preview behavior.
- [ ] Decide whether messaging and live broadcasting belong in later stages.
- [ ] Define feed ranking, user controls, sponsored-content disclosure, and anti-spam.
- [ ] Decide age policy, audience suitability, reporting, automated detection, human moderation, and appeals.
- [ ] Define user reputation/levels, if any, without exposing private history.

## Travel planning and records
- [ ] Define sources for maps, traffic, operating hours, prices, and availability.
- [ ] Define itinerary feasibility and custom-route behavior.
- [ ] Decide evidence levels and optional location verification.
- [ ] Define privacy defaults, sharing, correction, retention, and deletion.

## Operations and sustainability
- [ ] Estimate server, payment, security, moderation, support, and staffing costs.
- [ ] Define provider and customer support operations.
- [ ] Define sustainable revenue model and fairness safeguards.
- [ ] Set launch scope, validation milestones, and outcome metrics.
