# My Bali — Travel Journey, Evidence, and Community

**Status:** Exploratory concept; no final content or privacy policy approved.

## 1. Three distinct user activities
My Bali should not make every user contribution depend on a completed transaction. Different activities serve different purposes:

### A. Planning
A user who has not visited Bali may save places, build a wishlist, draft an itinerary, ask questions, or prepare for a future trip. A plan is not a completed experience and should never be displayed as one.

### B. Experience records
A user may record a place actually visited, an activity, a booking, or a completed service. The interface must show the evidence level accurately.

### C. Community discussion
Users may ask questions, exchange information, discuss destinations, and share opinions without claiming to have visited or purchased anything.

A future policy may place some restrictions on publishing certain experience-story formats without supporting evidence, but this must not block ordinary discussion or planning.

## 2. Travel timeline
A private timeline may show records by date, such as Monday's visit, Tuesday with no recorded activity, and Wednesday's restaurant visit. An empty day should remain empty; the system must not invent activity.

Possible record fields: date/time, place, route, activity, booking/transaction reference, photos, notes, status, and evidence source.

## 3. Evidence levels
Keep distinct:
- Confirmed payment/transaction.
- Confirmed booking.
- Provider-confirmed redemption or fulfilled service.
- User-recorded visit or location.
- User-authored story or opinion.
- Planned itinerary or wishlist.

Payment does not automatically prove attendance. A booking does not automatically prove completion. GPS does not prove purchase. A photo or story is not automatically independently verified.

## 4. Privacy controls
Users should be able to keep records private, share with selected people, or publish selected content publicly. Users should have meaningful controls to change visibility and understand what information is exposed. Exact defaults, retention, deletion, and location-sharing rules remain open.

## 5. Social connections
Potential capabilities include following providers, following other users, liking content, comments or discussion, sharing public pages/posts externally, and later messaging or live sessions. These are staged possibilities, not all launch requirements.

Public pages/posts should be shareable through ordinary web links so a recipient can preview useful public information. If deeper interaction requires the app, the reason and next step should be clear. Do not force a download merely to view a public web page if a safe web preview is feasible.

## 6. Public experience stories
Public stories may help other people discover real journeys. The product should distinguish verified facts from the author's opinions and should not imply that every saved plan was completed.

## 7. Safety, content suitability, and age
The founder does not currently imagine My Bali as a child-directed product. The intended audience, minimum age, age assurance, app-store age rating, and access restrictions remain to be decided with legal and platform requirements.

Bali travel imagery can include ordinary beachwear or swimwear in a legitimate travel context; it should not be automatically treated as sexual content. This is distinct from explicit sexual content, exploitation, non-consensual intimate imagery, or other prohibited material.

Violence, exploitation, harassment, scams, and other harmful content require clear policy and enforcement. Automated detection may assist but should not be treated as infallible; reporting, human review, appeals, and urgent escalation need design.

## 8. User levels and reputation
A possible level/profile concept based on real experiences remains an idea. It should reward trustworthy, useful participation rather than raw posting volume, spending, or popularity, and must not reveal private travel history.
