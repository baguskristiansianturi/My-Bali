# My Bali — Provider Ecosystem and Verification

**Status:** Working principles; detailed SOP and legal review required.

## 1. Direct-provider principle
My Bali aims to connect customers with the actual business or operator responsible for delivering the service. The founder wants to reduce dependence on unauthorized brokers, opaque resellers, and intermediaries who add substantial markups without clear service responsibility.

Eligible applicants may include an owner or a representative with verifiable authority. A person must not be presented as the actual operator if they only resell or pass leads. Authorized agents or distribution partners require an explicit policy and transparent disclosure; no blanket exception is approved.

## 2. Examples
Potential provider categories include attractions, beaches or managed tourism sites, cultural venues, restaurants, cafes, accommodation, transport, tours, activity and watersport operators, shops, and boutiques.

For a managed attraction such as Pantai Pandawa, the relevant manager should be able to establish authority to represent the attraction and maintain its official listing.

## 3. Early participation direction
The founder wants early registration to be easy and is exploring a no-upfront-fee approach for genuine providers to create and maintain a basic listing. This does not yet settle transaction commission, paid promotion, optional tools, or future commercial terms. Do not advertise a permanent free policy until confirmed.

## 4. Provider information
A provider page may contain:
- Verified business/attraction identity and operating authority.
- Location and access information.
- Actual services and facilities.
- Current prices, terms, operating hours, and availability.
- Photos and videos supplied by the provider.
- Dates or timestamps showing when media and key information were updated.
- Announcements, events, and time-limited offers.
- Booking conditions and customer support details.

Providers should be able to maintain accurate information without requiring a new post for every permanent detail change. The platform should preserve update history where useful.

## 5. Content and audience
A provider may publish updates from its official presence, build followers, and receive likes or engagement. Relevant updates may appear in users' feeds even if they do not follow the provider, based on user interests and behavior.

The ranking system must consider relevance, freshness, quality, repetition, and user controls. It must avoid allowing every feed slot to become an advertisement. Paid promotion, if introduced, must be clearly labeled.

## 6. Verification and quality are separate
Identity/authority verification does not guarantee service quality. A future SOP should separately evaluate provider reliability, complaints, fulfillment, accuracy of pricing, and policy compliance.

Potential enforcement steps: receive evidence, investigate proportionately, notify the provider where appropriate, request correction, warn, restrict, suspend, or remove under published criteria. A single negative review should not automatically cause removal. Appeals and fair treatment need to be designed.

## 7. Transaction and ticket validation concept
For supported providers, a possible flow is:
1. Provider configures ticket/service, price, validity, schedule, and inventory.
2. User selects the offer and required schedule.
3. My Bali processes payment through an integrated payment service.
4. Booking and transaction records are synchronized to the provider's management view.
5. User receives a secure QR or equivalent credential.
6. Provider scans it at entry/service delivery.
7. System validates the booking and marks it redeemed/fulfilled.

This is an illustrative target flow. It is not an existing capability. Security, duplicate scans, refunds, partial redemption, connectivity loss, staff permissions, and settlement require detailed design.

## 8. Provider categories and onboarding SOP
A future SOP should define category-specific evidence, identity checks, business authority, permits where applicable, listing review, updates, complaints, and ongoing verification. Collect only information proportionate to the risk and legal requirements.
