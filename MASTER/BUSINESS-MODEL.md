# My Bali — Business Model (Working Draft)

**Status:** Exploratory model, not approved financial policy.

## 1. Value created
My Bali aims to create value for several connected groups:
- **Travelers and residents:** discover places, plan trips, compare useful information, book supported services, and keep travel memories.
- **Direct providers:** gain visibility, publish accurate information, reach interested users, receive bookings, and manage service fulfillment.
- **Local ecosystem:** improve discoverability and reduce avoidable friction between customers and the businesses that actually deliver services.

## 2. Early participation principle
The founder wants joining to be easy for both ordinary users and genuine providers. An early concept is to allow a verified provider to create a listing and manage its basic information without an upfront listing fee. This is a founder direction to explore, not a published commercial promise or complete fee policy.

The following must be treated separately:
- Account registration.
- Creating and maintaining a provider profile.
- Publishing ordinary updates.
- Optional promotional placement.
- Booking and payment processing.
- Commission or transaction fees.
- Additional paid tools or services.

No final decision has been made on which of these will be free, paid, or subject to limits.

## 3. Provider value proposition
A genuine provider may be able to:
- Register the actual attraction or business it operates or is authorized to represent.
- Publish accurate prices, operating hours, service details, photos, and videos.
- Date or identify when information and media were last updated.
- Publish announcements, events, new experiences, and limited-time offers.
- Build a follower audience and receive engagement.
- Receive bookings and transaction records through supported workflows.
- Validate eligible tickets or bookings at the location.

## 4. User value proposition
A user may be able to:
- Browse useful destination and provider information even when the community is still small.
- Save places and build plans before traveling.
- Follow providers and discover relevant updates.
- Book and pay for supported services.
- Keep booking, payment, and experience records.
- Share eligible experiences while controlling privacy.

## 5. Booking and fulfillment concept
Illustrative flow for an attraction ticket:
1. A verified attraction manager publishes ticket information and a promotion.
2. A user discovers the offer and checks its conditions.
3. The user selects a date/time or applicable visit window.
4. The user pays through a supported My Bali checkout.
5. My Bali creates a booking/transaction record and makes the relevant reservation data available to the provider.
6. The user receives a scannable booking credential, such as a QR code.
7. At arrival, the provider scans the code and validates the ticket.
8. The booking status is updated to reflect redemption or entry.

This flow is an intended example, not a currently available service. Payment provider, settlement, refunds, chargebacks, offline validation, fraud controls, and provider reconciliation need separate design.

## 6. Revenue and sustainability
The founder recognizes that a mature platform needs to fund infrastructure, servers, security, support, operations, and staff. Revenue must eventually support sustainable operations.

Potential revenue mechanisms may include transaction commission, optional provider tools, clearly disclosed advertising or sponsored placements, or other services. None has been selected. A revenue model must not undermine the platform's direct-provider principle or mislead users about ranking and recommendations.

## 7. Marketplace integrity
My Bali aims to reduce reliance on unauthorized brokers and opaque markups. It should show the actual provider, clarify who fulfills the service, and make prices and conditions understandable. Any permitted agency or distribution relationship must be explicitly authorized and transparently represented.

## 8. Open financial questions
- Is listing and basic provider account management free permanently or only during an introductory period?
- Will My Bali charge commission on completed transactions?
- Who bears payment gateway fees?
- How will refunds, cancellations, disputes, and failed fulfillment be handled?
- When and how are providers paid?
- What operational service levels can the business afford?
- How will sponsored content be labeled and ranked?
- What minimum volume is needed to cover server, security, staff, and customer support costs?
