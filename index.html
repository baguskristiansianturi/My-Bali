The idea behind My Bali

More than finding a place. A better way to connect with Bali.

My Bali is being imagined as one connected experience: discover somewhere worth going, understand who provides the service, plan how to get there, and keep the memories that matter.

⌖

Discover

Explore beaches, restaurants, activities, stays, shops, and local experiences across Bali.

↗

Plan your journey

Bring places and interests together into a journey that considers time, distance, and practical details.

◎

Connect directly

Find genuine local providers and, as the platform develops, book supported services in one place.

Your Bali, your story

A journey that stays with you.

From the first idea to the places you actually visit, My Bali aims to help people plan with confidence and keep their experiences in a way that feels personal.

✓

✓

What matters to us

Built on trust, usefulness, and local connection.

The details will evolve. These principles guide the decisions we make along the way.

01
Real providers, clear identity

Help users understand who is actually responsible for a service.

02
Transparent information

Keep prices, availability, and service details clear and appropriately verified.

03
Privacy by choice

People should have meaningful control over their personal travel records and stories.

04
Grow with the community

Improve through real use, listening, responsible operations, and continuous learning.

The beginning of My Bali
